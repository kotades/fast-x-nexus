## Design Specifications
*   **Theme**: Light Industrial Monolith (high contrast, clean, sharp, rectangular).
*   **Dimensions**: Height 64px, full-width.
*   **Colors**:
    *   `Background`: `#FFFFFF`
    *   `Borders`: 1px `#E5E7EB` (bottom border), with a top accent line of 4px brand forest green `#347227`.
    *   `Typography/Text`: `#111827` (primary headings/text), `#4B5563` (muted text).
*   **Brand Mark (Left)**: "FAST X NEXUS" where:
    *   "FAST X" is in bold forest green (`#347227`) text.
    *   "NEXUS" has the letter "E" styled customly as three horizontal gold (`#E4A800`) bars (matching the brand logo).
*   **Center Navigation Tabs**:
    *   Items: "Dashboard", "Bookings", "Tracking", "Reports".
    *   Style: Clean text, medium size, with active indicators matching the brand forest green (`#347227`).
*   **Right Side Actions**:
    *   User avatar circle.
    *   Wallet balance chip showing `$2,450.00` in bold, with a gold (`#E4A800`) background or border.
*   **Responsiveness**: Hamburger menu icon appears on mobile screens, navigation tabs collapse.
*   **Geometry**: Strictly square/rectangular corners throughout (border-radius: 0px or 4px soft corner option if needed, but defaults to sharp for industrial feel). No drop shadows.
