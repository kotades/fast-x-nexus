## Design Specifications
*   **Theme**: Light Industrial Monolith (high contrast, clean, sharp, rectangular).
*   **Overlay/Backdrop**: Light, semi-transparent backdrop overlay covering the whole screen.
*   **Modal Dimensions**: Centered floating panel with `#FFFFFF` background.
*   **Borders**: 
    *   3px brand forest green `#347227` top border accent.
    *   1px solid grey `#E5E7EB` border on the left, right, and bottom sides.
*   **Typography/Text**:
    *   Title: Bold dark charcoal text `#111827`.
    *   Description: Muted grey text `#4B5563`.
*   **Action Buttons (Bottom Row)**:
    *   **Primary Button**: Brand forest green `#347227` fill, white text, sharp corners, no drop shadows.
    *   **Secondary Button**: Transparent background, 1px solid grey `#D1D5DB` or `#E5E7EB` border, dark text, sharp corners.
*   **Keyboard Accessibility**: ESC key dismiss indicator, focus trap to lock focus on modal when open.
*   **Geometry**: Strictly square/rectangular corners throughout (rounded-none).
